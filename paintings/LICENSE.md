# Paintings

The images in this folder (`*.png`) were painted in the simulator by AI models (Claude and GPT) working from scripts, some from reference photos. They are dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The painting scripts (`*.js`) are code and fall under the repository's MIT licence.

Reference photos are not included. The Okefenokee paintings follow a public-domain U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service photo by Steve Brooks (fws.gov/media/sunrise-okefenokee-refuge).
