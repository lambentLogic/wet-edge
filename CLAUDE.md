Read AGENTS.md first: how to run, paint with scripts, and every tool, action and knob. It is generated (`node tools/agents-doc.mjs`) from src/actions.js, src/params.js and src/knob-docs.js; after changing those, regenerate it and run `node tools/measure.mjs api`.

The painting journal and source notes live in notes/, a private repo of its own (lambentLogic/hyperreal-watercolor-notes, cloned there and ignored here; commit and push it separately).

Before painting, read notes/journal.md (what has worked, what the painter has said); after, add to it (`sim.note(text)` in a script, or `node tools/note.mjs`).
